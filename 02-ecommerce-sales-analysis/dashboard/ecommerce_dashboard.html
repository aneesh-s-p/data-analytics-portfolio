Key insights and recommendations

  • Second purchase is the biggest gap. 69% of customers buy once, and only about 2.4% of a cohort returns the next month. Test a 30-day reminder or discount.
  • Channel quality is flat. Add acquisition cost data before shifting budget between channels.
  • Margin leaders: Blazers & Jackets (62%), Accessories (60%) and Suits (60%). Tops & Tees is lowest at 44%.
  • Concentration risk. Three countries drive 71% of sales, so test mid-size markets such as the UK, Germany and France.
