| Project | Question | Tools | Headline result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [01 Customer Churn Analysis](01-customer-churn-analysis) | Who is leaving a telecom company and why? | Python, pandas, scikit-learn | Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% vs 2.8% on two-year plans; models reach ROC-AUC 0.84 |
